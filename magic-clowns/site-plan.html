Solcito y Alejito Site Plan

Jose De Francesco

WDD 131-A2

Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to establish a professional digital presence for the duo 'Solcito and Alejito,' offering an interactive catalog of their magic and comedy shows. The platform aims to simplify event planning for clients by providing clear information about their services, trustworthy testimonials, and a direct contact channel, transforming the search for entertainment into a magical and efficient experience.

Audience

Parents with children aged 0-12, school event organizers and party venue owners looking for a professional clown and magic show.

Dynamic elements

I will use an array of objects to manage the different show packages. I will implement JavaScript functions to filter the services by type (show, magic, balloons, or games) and use DOM events to display additional details when each service is clicked.

Typography

Heading Font: Bangers

I chose 'Bangers' because it has a comic and circus-style aesthetic that perfectly matches the energy of a clown and magic duo. It is bold, fun, and highly visible.

Paragraph Font: Quicksand

I chose 'Quicksand' because its rounded edges make it look friendly and accessible, which is ideal for an audience composed of parents and children.

Content

Home page

Main Message: "More than a show, a magical memory."
Summary: The home page will feature a vibrant hero image of the duo, a brief "About Us" section highlighting their 10+ years of experience, and featured testimonials from happy parents.
Images: High-quality photos of magic tricks, balloon twisting, and smiling children.

Shows & Services Page

Summary: This page will display an interactive catalog of three main services: 1. Magic Gala: 45 minutes of stage magic. 2. Funny Birthday: Clowning, interactive games, and balloon animals. 3. School Special: Educational humor for large groups.
Data Structure: Each service will be a card dynamically generated by JavaScript from an array of objects.
